Scope: `f70bac1` (CLI registry routing), Grok usage in `742ae0f`, and the ACP configuration they exercise. `grok` is absent on PATH. `grok --help`, `grok agent --help`, and authenticated execution are **unverifiable here**. The prior README's live-smoke assertions were not evidence collected in this audit and have been removed from its current implementation paragraph.
